Privacy Policy

Universe Daily iOS App · Version 1.3 · Effective November 19, 2026

Summary

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. The app connects to the internet to retrieve science and space content, and a few features use your approximate area without asking for your location. Nothing about you is kept by us.

Data Collection

The Universe Daily iOS app does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users. No personally identifiable information (such as names, emails, or device identifiers) is gathered, and there are no accounts.

Internet Connection

The app requests content directly from the organizations that publish it, such as NASA, NOAA, CelesTrak, The Space Devs, xkcd, Wikipedia and the news sites shown in the app, with N2YO and Where the ISS At as backups for space station positions. Maps in the app come from Apple Maps. Like any internet request, these providers receive your device's IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies.

Approximate Location

The light pollution map and the aurora forecast start near where you are, without asking for location access. To do this, the app asks Universe Daily's own service (universe-api.kportal.net, hosted on Cloudflare), which estimates your general area, roughly the nearest city, from the IP address of the request and sends it back. The service does not store or log this information.

The app keeps the most recent area on your device so the map can start in the right place next time. It never uses GPS or your precise location.

When the public launch database is busy, the app may also get the launch schedule through the same service. These requests contain no personal information and are not stored.

Photos and Calendar

If you choose Save as Wallpaper, the app asks permission to add pictures to your photo library. That permission only lets the app add pictures; it cannot see your photos. If you add a launch or eclipse to your calendar, the app opens the iOS calendar editor, nothing is added unless you tap Add, and the app cannot read your calendar. Pictures and comics you share go only where you send them with the iOS share sheet.

Third-Party Services

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising networks, or social media platforms. Some features show web pages from other sites inside the app, such as the light pollution map from lightpollutionmap.info, NASA's Eyes on the Solar System and Deep Space Network pages, and videos from YouTube and Vimeo. Those pages are run by their owners, may use their own cookies, and are covered by their own privacy policies. The app blocks the light pollution map's ads, trackers and pop-ups.

Universe Daily on the Web

The Universe Daily website (space.kportal.net) has no accounts, and no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies of its own. Your preferences (such as the last Night Sky section you viewed), recently loaded content and, if you share it, your location are saved only in your own browser's local storage. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

To show live content, your browser requests it directly from the content providers (for example NASA, NOAA, CelesTrak, The Space Devs and the Spaceflight News API). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts and code libraries load from Google Fonts, jsDelivr and unpkg. Like any website, each of these receives your IP address with the request.

If you choose "Center on my area" or "Chance where I am", your browser asks for permission first. Your location is then used in your browser to work out aurora chances and to center the embedded light pollution map, so lightpollutionmap.info receives it. It is never sent to us.

The website also shows pages from other sites, such as the light pollution map, NASA's Eyes on the Solar System and videos from YouTube and Vimeo. Unlike the app, the website shows these pages exactly as their owners publish them, including the light pollution map's own advertising, and they are covered by their owners' privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

If future updates require changes to how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated and a new version number will be issued.
