Privacy Policy

Universe Daily iOS App · Version 1.1 · Effective September 1, 2025

Summary

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. The app connects to the internet only to retrieve science and space content for display.

Data Collection

The Universe Daily iOS app does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users. No personally identifiable information (such as names, emails, or device identifiers) is gathered beyond any data collected by content providers used by said app to provide content.

Internet Connection

The app connects to the internet solely for the purpose of retrieving and displaying relevant data (such as space- or science-related information). This connection is used only to provide content within the app.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising networks, or social media platforms.

Universe Daily on the Web

The Universe Daily website works the same way. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Your preferences (such as the last Night Sky section you viewed) and recently loaded content are saved only in your own browser's local storage, and you can clear them at any time through your browser settings.

To show live content, your browser requests it directly from the content providers (for example NASA, NOAA, The Space Devs and the Spaceflight News API). Like any website, those providers receive your IP address with each request. If you choose "Center on my area" or "Chance where I am", your location is used in your browser only and is never sent to us.

Changes to This Policy

If future updates require changes to how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated and a new version number will be issued.
